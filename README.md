## 🧱 Field summary

- `domain` — Fully qualified domain name.
- `status` — Current acquisition state for the domain in the public extract.
- `purchase_price` — Visible purchase price when available.
- `renewal_price` — Visible renewal price when available.
- `attractiveness` — Composite naming score used as a decision-support signal.
- `demand` — Relative buyer-pressure score when available.
- `length` — Character count without the TLD.
- `registrar` — Registrar name when known.
- `created_at` — Creation timestamp when known.
- `expires_at` — Expiry timestamp when known.

## ⚠️ Methodology and caveats

This set is entirely made up of one-word .boats domains. The naming style ranges from category-defining terms like luxury.boats and trade.boats to broader brand terms like brief.boats, inspire.boats, and unusual.boats. That makes the selection relevant to both marine businesses seeking a direct niche signal and buyers assessing resale potential in a specialized extension. When comparing these domains, start with commercial clarity: exact-match marine terms usually communicate purpose fastest, while broader words may offer a more flexible brand. The median ask is 91.89, so price discipline matters less than choosing a word with strong recall, clean meaning, and realistic long-term fit within the .boats extension.

- Exact-match marine terms can signal intent faster
- Broad words may be brandable but need stronger fit
- Median ask is 91.89 across 12,444 .boats domains
- Check renewal economics before judging a low ask
